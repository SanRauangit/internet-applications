Description

Chapter 1 ("Owl Post"): Harry spends another dreadful summer with the Dursleys, secretly completing his wizarding schoolwork at night. On his thirteenth birthday, midnight owl deliveries bring him warm cards and thoughtful gifts from his friends Ron, Hermione, and Hagrid.

Chapter 2 ("Aunt Marge's Big Mistake"): Vernon's spiteful sister, Marge, visits and relentlessly insults Harry and his late parents. Pushed past his breaking point, Harry accidentally inflates her like a balloon with accidental magic and runs away into the night with his trunk.

Chapter 3 ("The Knight Bus"): Stranded in the dark after fleeing Privet Drive, Harry encounters a sinister black dog in the shadows before unintentionally summoning the magical, triple-decker Knight Bus. He learns about the escaped convict Sirius Black during the journey and arrives at the Leaky Cauldron, where Minister Cornelius Fudge surprisingly excuses his illegal magic.

Chapter 4 ("The Leaky Cauldron"): Enjoying a brief period of freedom in Diagon Alley, Harry buys his school supplies and reunites with Ron and Hermione. Before returning to Hogwarts, Harry overhears the Weasleys discussing the grave danger he is in, revealing that Sirius Black escaped Azkaban specifically to target him.
